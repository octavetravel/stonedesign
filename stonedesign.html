Why we do it

A countertop outlives the kitchen it's built for.

The Toledo family has spent a working lifetime around stone — from the quarry countries of South America to the shop floor here in Georgia. That background changes how we see a slab. It isn't inventory. It's a single, unrepeatable piece of the earth that a family will gather around for the next thirty years.

So we refuse to treat fabrication like a commodity. We'd rather be the shop that knows your slab by name than the one that moves the most square feet. That belief runs through every cut we make — and it's the reason our customers keep sending their neighbors.

— Regis & Divina Toledo Owners, Stone Design

How we're different

One roof. One crew. One point of accountability.

Most countertop "fabricators" sell you a four-inch sample, then ship your job to a shop you'll never see. When something's off, the finger-pointing starts. We built Stone Design to work the opposite way — every step stays with the same team.

01 — Selection

Pick your actual slab

Natural stone never repeats. You choose the exact slab your counters are cut from — veining, movement and all — and we tag it in your name. No guessing from a sample chip.

02 — Precision

Templated to the millimeter

We digitally template your space on-site, so seams fall where they should, overhangs land right, and the finished tops fit the first time — not after a second trip.

03 — Craft

Cut and finished in-house

Your slab is fabricated and hand-finished in our Norcross shop. Our polishing protects the stone's structure instead of burning it smooth — so the edges hold their depth for years.

04 — Accountability

Installed and backed 100%

The people who cut your stone are the people who set it. Materials and workmanship carry a 100% warranty, and there's one number to call if you ever need us. No subcontractor maze.
